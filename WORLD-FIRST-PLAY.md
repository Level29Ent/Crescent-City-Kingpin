# Explore first, interact on-site

The city map sets directions. It never accepts a mission, buys an address, or teleports the player. Existing campaigns and active stages remain valid; no reset is required.

## Playing

- **M / MAP**: four pause sections — City Map, Objective, Progress, Settings. The map opens on your current objective when one exists. Otherwise it shows available contacts. The address directory is collapsed until requested.
- **Cyan signed door**: approach on foot and use **E / INTERACT**. Walk through the furnished room to the attendant at the back desk; interact again for local work or services. Leave through the teal exit. Merely viewing or entering a property never buys it.
- **Gold street ring**: meet the visible host on foot and interact. Race marshals, waiting taxi passengers, the parade marshal, the fishing attendant and the threatened witness live in the city, not in a global launcher.
- **Contact conversation**: one offer at a time. Some contacts have several local topics. Choose an approach where offered. Story unlocks, funds, crew requirements, cooldowns and the single-active-activity rule still apply.
- **Work cargo**: accepting at the pickup desk collects the cargo once. Walk out and follow GPS. Enter the destination building and interact at its back desk to deliver. Payment still requires completion; map selection and travel grant nothing.
- **Services**: only the current address's services are available. The clinic treats you; the tailor/barber handles appearance; the chop shop sells/deploys/upgrades cars; the precinct handles impounds; each business handles its own purchase/upkeep/laundering; landmark heist planning happens inside that landmark. Mama Nola's back desk provides gear/import dispatch and home services. Furniture/visual changes appear on re-entry/deployment as before.
- **Ambient requests**: city time makes relief/witness offers available; it no longer silently assigns an activity. Visit the contact to volunteer.
- **Objective**: current stage first, with no conflicting suggestion to accept another job. Abandonment pays nothing and refunds nothing; supported checkpoint retries retain their existing $50 cost.

Interiors remain the game's existing safe, instanced visits; they are not newly streamed GTA V interiors. Outdoor time/combat remains paused inside. This changes where gameplay is accepted and services are accessed, not the game's engine or world scale.

## Verification

Domain tests cover physical-distance/desk/vehicle gates, all activity families and property addresses, fees, cooldowns, choices, progression, one active activity, one-time delivery payment, non-mutating GPS/eligibility checks, and live objective markers. Local browser checks exercise physical entry and walking to the clinic/diner desks, acceptance/pickup/delivery, the parade marshal and the simplified pause UI. Test campaigns seed street positions for independent destination checks; they do not prove a full cross-city driving route or every mission completion.
