# City and fleet refresh

- Death now resets campaign heat and HUD stars to zero, clears police intervention/timer and roadblock, dismisses pursuing officers without kill rewards, stops sirens and cancels active work. A death reset is not counted as outrunning police. The existing 10% clean-cash penalty remains. Economy/pursuit updates pause during the five-second recovery; respawning restores stamina. Saving during that animation resumes at spawn with full health rather than one HP at the fight.
- Six new public, instanced rooms bring the total to 47: Basin Street Patisserie, Crescent Post Office, Levee Auto Parts, Royal Street Gallery, Marigny Guest House and Canal Neon Arcade. Each has a signed collision-free facade, map/directory entry, themed furnishings, service desk and once-per-save supplies. Arcade cabinets are visual props, not playable minigames; visits do not grant ownership.
- Three new purchasable/storable vehicles bring the garage catalog to 13: Frenchmen Open-Top Roadster ($3,700), Garden District Estate Wagon ($2,900) and Bayou Trail Utility 4×4 ($5,600). Their capacity/speed/armor differ. All procedural cars gain window pillars, handles, mirrors, panel seams, grille, plates, markers, exhausts, alloy spokes and polished paint/chrome. The motorcycle gains mirrors, engine fins and exhausts. Moving traffic and parked vehicles use a wider mix. Geometry/materials remain cached; no external model downloads or new dependencies.
- Three clean courier jobs link all six new destinations. Legal work pays an early-delivery bonus of up to 20%, proportional to time remaining. Art recovery retains its fixed dirty-cash/heat reward. Pickups, delivery interaction, expiry, onboarding, cooldown and single-payment gates remain.

Validation: death/heat persistence, garage purchase/cargo/save rules, all rooms' walkable supply/desk/exit aisles, distinct usable facade entrances and work route progression are exercised by the Node tests. Preview playtesting status is recorded in the pull request. Long-chase balance and low-end GPU performance still need playtesting.
