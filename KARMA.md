# Karma dispensary

Karma is a new free public interior, not a replacement for Magazine Street Herbalist or a purchasable property. Find **Karma** in Empire → Buildings or City Map → Buildings & homes. Its signed facade is near the starting French Quarter area (door approach x=5, z=-16). Walk there on foot and press E / INTERACT to enter.

The stylized interior is inspired by the supplied shop photographs: matte black walls and ceiling, pale veined tile, black-framed glass display cases, long wall shelves, backlit timber cubbies, green Karma rugs, rectangular ceiling lights, and skeleton decorations. The back wall has timber slats, a paneled back-room door and a dark service window. Photographs are references, not embedded textures; all art is generated locally.

The centre aisle stays clear. E at the rug emblem (x=0, z=1) claims the existing once-per-save first-aid supply. The rear service desk opens Market; the teal threshold or Exit Building returns to the street. Existing prices, properties, and economy are unchanged. The visible back-room door is decorative, not another enterable room. The front wall and ceiling remain camera-safe for the existing third-person view.

Checks cover all 41 entrances, walking from exit through supplies to the desk and back, case collision, single-claim supplies, old saves, glass/lighting meshes, and generated-texture disposal. Browser verification is recorded on the PR.
